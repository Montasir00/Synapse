# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
High-performing individuals ("Operators") who manage and optimize personal productivity, financial capital, and physical performance.

## Product Purpose
A unified "Neural Dashboard" serving as a central executive command center that bridges disparate life systems, consolidating task protocols, financial auditing, trade analysis, and physical exertion into a premium, immersive, and ultra-responsive interface.

## Positioning
A consolidated personal optimization tool built specifically for disciplined users who demand high-fidelity metrics and latency-free auditing across their entire life stack.

## Operating Context
Web and mobile PWA viewports. Highly optimized for quick daily glances (Dailies/Protocols), detailed financial ledger audits, post-trade reviews, and physical status logs.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Tasks**: Manual logging categorized into Dailies (Protocols), Strategic Epics (Long-term), and Persistent Notes.
- **Finances**: Expense ledger with merchant-to-category learning, month-over-month cashflow forecasting, and aggregate spending metrics.
- **Loans**: Debt registry for logging and managing settled vs active transactions.
- **Trade Tracker**: Post-trade analysis platform supporting manual updates and API-based trade synchronization.
- **Exercises**: Biological monitoring for physical exertion and active recovery.
- **Sync & Offline**: Real-time Firebase Firestore synchronization; offline-first capability through custom Service Worker caching.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: Synapse
- **Aesthetic**: Dark "Obsidian" neo-minimalist styling with clean visual components and smooth, zero-friction gesture physics.

## Evidence on Hand
Incumbent React 19 + Vite 6 + Tailwind 4 codebase with a fully functional layout across all six primary views (Dashboard, Tasks, Expenses, Loans, Trade Tracker, and Exercises).

## Product Principles
- **Real-Time Cohesion**: Consolidated metrics must load fast without redundant network requests or layout latency.
- **Tactile Precision**: Interface controls must provide clear touch targets, responsive state indicators, and seamless motion feedback.
- **Fidelity over Clutter**: High-density data views (ledgers, charts) must remain readable, clean, and highly scannable.
